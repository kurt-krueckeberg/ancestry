# Indiana Land Records

and the Indiana Archives and Records Adminstration’s website has information on [Land Records](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/)
that includes these topics:

- [Land Records at the Indiana State Archives](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#Land%20Records%20at%20the%20Indiana%20State%20Archives)
- [Federal Land Offices](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#Federal%20Land%20Office%20Records)
- [Land Office Indexes](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#Land%20Office%20Indexes)
- [State Lands including Indianapolis Donation](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#State%20Lands)
- [GLO Plat Maps](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#GLO%20Plat%20Maps)
- [Native American Land Records](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#Native%20American%20Land%20Records)
- [Tippecanoe Battle Ground Plat Map](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#Tippecanoe%20Battle%20Ground%20Plat%20Map)
- [Hoosier Homestead Awards](https://www.in.gov/iara/divisions/state-archives/collections/land-records/#Hoosier%20Homestead%20Awards)

# Viewing PLSS township, range and section

Two online map apps that allow you to view the Public Land Survey System’s townships, ranges and sections are
[Bureau of Land Management Eastern States Public Land
Survey System Townships](https://gbp-blm-egis.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/f4338afccbde49b5b06ad7da880046e6_7/explore) and [Arcgis PLSS Map Viewer](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/View/index.html?appid=019dd6f39fda4d3b811abfab0878b63b)

Steps to use the [Bureau of Land Management Eastern States Public Land
Survey System Townships](https://gbp-blm-egis.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/f4338afccbde49b5b06ad7da880046e6_7/explore):

1. Use the filter icon in the toolbar and choose:
  1. State Abbreviation
  2. Township Number
  3. Range Number
